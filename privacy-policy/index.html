WHO OWES ME?

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Who Owes Me? is a personal debt and IOU tracking application created by Mahala Mzati Mkwepu.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Who Owes Me? application.

1. Information You Provide

When you first use Who Owes Me?, the App asks you to provide a username. The username is used to personalize your experience within the App and is stored locally on your device.

The App may also allow you to enter information such as:

  • Names of people who owe you
  • Amounts and currencies
  • Debt or IOU descriptions
  • Dates and due dates
  • Notes
  • Payment information
  • Debt status and payment history

This information is stored locally on your device.

2. No Online Account or Developer Database

Who Owes Me? does not require you to create an online account or sign in to use the App.

The App does not operate a developer-controlled cloud database for storing your username, debts, IOUs, notes, or payment information.

Your information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer's servers as part of the App's normal operation.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information you enter into the App is used to provide and personalize the App's features, including:

  • Personalizing the App with your username
  • Displaying people who owe you
  • Tracking debts and IOUs
  • Calculating outstanding and paid amounts
  • Providing debt statistics
  • Generating reminders
  • Managing payment history
  • Providing other features within the App

4. Data Sharing

Who Owes Me? does not sell, rent, or share your locally stored information with the developer or third parties as part of its normal operation.

If you choose to share a debt reminder through WhatsApp, SMS, or another application, the information you choose to share is transferred to that application by your action. That information is then handled according to the third party's privacy policy and terms.

5. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Who Owes Me? may use your device's notification functionality to remind you about debts, due dates, or other information you have entered into the App.

6. Data Security

Because your information is stored locally on your device, its security also depends on the security of your device.

We recommend protecting your device with a PIN, password, fingerprint, facial recognition, or another available security feature.

The developer does not have access to your locally stored username, debt records, notes, or payment information through the normal operation of the App.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete individual debt records through the App where the relevant functionality is available.

You can also clear the App's stored data through your device settings or uninstall the App.

Because the App does not maintain a cloud account containing your information, the developer does not have a copy of your locally stored data to delete.

8. Children's Privacy

Who Owes Me? is not specifically directed toward children. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Applications

The App may allow you to share information using applications installed on your device, such as messaging applications.

Any information you choose to share through another application is subject to that application's privacy policy and terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be made available at the published Privacy Policy URL. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will also be updated when appropriate.
